## 🖋️ HOW TO PUBLISH ON DEV.to

**Time: 30 minutes to write/publish**

**Steps:**

1. **Create DEV.to Account:**
   - Go to: https://dev.to
   - Sign up with GitHub (easiest)
   - Connect GitHub account

2. **Write Article:**
   - Click "Create Post"
   - Use Markdown
   - Paste your content

3. **Add Metadata:**
   - **Title:** "Zero-Downtime Kubernetes Deployments on AWS EKS: Blue-Green Strategy with ArgoCD & GitOps"
   - **Cover Image:** Find on Unsplash (search "Kubernetes", "AWS", "deployment")
   - **Tags:** `#kubernetes #aws #devops #argocd #gitops #eks`
   - **Description:** Brief 1-line summary
   - **Series:** Optional (good if planning multiple articles)

4. **Preview & Publish:**
   - Click "Preview"
   - Check formatting
   - Click "Publish"

5. **Share:**
   - Copy URL
   - Share on LinkedIn: "Just published my first technical article: [URL] #Kubernetes #AWS #DevOps"
   - Share on Twitter
   - Share in relevant Slack communities
